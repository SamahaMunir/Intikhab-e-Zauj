Intikhab-e-Zauj

A guided, staff-mediated matrimonial platform built for Falah Khandan Center — bringing our matchmaking online, with trust and modesty at its heart.

What this is

The same trusted matchmaking — now online.

For years the Center has helped families find suitable rishtas by hand — on paper, through trusted staff. Intikhab-e-Zauj takes that exact process and puts it on a website, so we can help more families, keep records safely, and never lose the human trust that makes our work special.

Nothing about our values changes. Staff still review every profile, still guide every proposal. The website simply makes the work faster, safer, and easier to manage.

What it does

Everything the Center does, in one place.

Verified profiles

Every applicant's profile is reviewed and approved by staff before anyone can see it. No fake or unchecked profiles.

Smart matching

The system suggests compatible profiles using a clear 100-point score — caste, family, education, and preferences. Staff always review before forwarding.

Privacy for women

A woman's photo stays hidden (blurred) until she shows interest. Only then is it shown, and only to that one family.

Guided proposals

When both sides are interested, staff forward the proposal and connect the families — a supervised, respectful conversation.

Counselling

Families can request pre- and post-marriage counselling directly, and staff assign a counsellor.

Secure payments

The yearly fee is paid safely online or by bank transfer, then verified by staff — all recorded in one place.

Why it is different

Not like the other apps.

Most matchmaking apps just put two strangers in a chat and walk away. Ours is built the way our families actually want it.

Staff stay in the loop
A trained person reviews every profile and guides every proposal — just like a traditional rishta, but organised and able to help many more families.
Modesty & Islamic values
Built around our values from the start — privacy, family involvement, and respectful, supervised communication.
Women's privacy protected
Photos are never shown openly. They stay hidden until the woman herself agrees — a protection almost no other platform offers.
Clear, honest matching
The compatibility score can be explained to any family — no hidden "algorithm," nothing forced. Families always decide.
Made for us
In our language, our pricing, our context — not a foreign app that doesn't understand desi families.
How the website works

Two sides working together.

Families use one side of the website; our staff manage everything from the other. Here is the full journey, start to finish.

A

The family's side

  1. Register & pay the yearly fee to join.
  2. Complete the profile — details, preferences, and a photo.
  3. Wait for approval — staff check the profile first.
  4. See suggested matches once approved.
  5. Show interest in a profile they like.
  6. Talk, once connected — a supervised conversation after both sides agree.
B

Our staff's side

  1. Review new profiles and approve or reject them.
  2. Check the suggested matches the system creates.
  3. Forward good proposals to the other family.
  4. Watch over the conversation to keep it respectful.
  5. Connect the families when both are interested.
  6. Handle counselling & payments as requests come in.
Staff training — the daily tasks

The few things you will do every day.

You don't need to understand the whole system. Learn these tasks well and you can run everything. Take your time — practise each one until it feels easy.

1

Log in

Open the website and sign in with your own staff account. Apna account use karein — kisi aur ko password na batayein.

2

Review a new profile

Open a pending profile, read the details, then Approve or Reject. Check the details look real and complete first.

3

View profiles & matches

Open any profile to see its details and the compatible matches the system suggests.

4

Forward a proposal

When a match looks good, send (forward) the proposal to the other family through the system.

5

Watch the messages

Keep an eye on the conversation between families and make sure it stays respectful.

6

Connect the families

When both sides are interested, contact both families to take things forward. Dono ghar raazi hon to aage baat karwayein.

7

Counselling & payments

Assign a counsellor when someone requests it, and confirm a payment once you have verified it.

If you get stuck

It's completely normal at first. Don't worry about breaking anything — nothing is deleted by accident. If you're unsure about any step, call for help before continuing. Ghabrayein nahi — pehle poochein, phir karein.

Points for everyone

Keeping families' trust safe.

Families trust us with private information. These simple rules protect them — and us.

  • Keep your login private. Never share your password or let someone else use your account.
  • Check before you approve. A careful look at each profile keeps the platform honest.
  • Never share photos or contacts outside the system. Especially women's photos — this is the whole promise we make.
  • Be respectful and confidential. Everything you see is private family information. It stays between the family and the Center.
  • When in doubt, ask. A quick question is always better than a wrong action.
