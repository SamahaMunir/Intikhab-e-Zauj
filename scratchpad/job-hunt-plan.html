The strategy

Three pillars, run every week.

You don't get hired by doing one thing hard once. You get hired by running three engines steadily until one converts. Keep all three alive weekly — never drop the daily habit for a big push.

Pillar 1

Get sharp

Rebuild the coding muscle (no AI), grind interview reps, and close the AWS / SQL / system-design gaps enough to speak to them.

Pillar 2

Be visible

LinkedIn: comment daily, post 1–2×/week. Recruiters come to active, credible profiles. Your shipped project is the magnet.

Pillar 3

Apply relentlessly

5 tailored applications a day, 4 days a week. Volume with aim — entry/associate, remote, your stack. Log everything.

The 16 weeks

Three phases: Sharpen → Volume → Convert.

Weeks 1–4
Sep 7 – Oct 4

Phase 1 · Sharpen & set the habit

  • Rebuild coding-without-AI muscle: LeetCode Easy daily, then Medium
  • Lock the daily rhythm (below) so it runs on autopilot
  • Take your first AI screens (micro1, etc.) once you feel sharp — banked, reusable
  • Finish any profile/resume polish so everything is consistent
Aim: habit locked · ~40 applications · 1–2 AI screens passed
Weeks 5–10
Oct 5 – Nov 15

Phase 2 · Volume & first interviews

  • Push applications hard — this is the volume block
  • Convert screens into first-round interviews; do mock interviews weekly
  • Keep learning: one system-design basic + a few SQL/AWS concepts each week
  • Warm the pipeline: recruiter outreach + reply to every response same day
Aim: ~120 applications total · 5–8 interviews · steady inbound
Weeks 11–16
Nov 16 – Jan 1

Phase 3 · Convert & close

  • Focus energy on live interview loops and warm leads
  • Deep-prep each company: role, stack, likely questions
  • Negotiate offers — never accept the first number blindly
  • Keep applying until an offer is signed, not just verbal
Aim: interview loops → 1–3 offers → signed by Jan 1
