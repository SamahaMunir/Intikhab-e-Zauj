In short

What we have agreed.

For now, while the Center arranges staff and the website is not yet opened to the public, the developer will keep the website running for a reduced monthly fee. When families begin using the website, the fee returns to the full amount. The details are below, each explained simply.

Point 1

Monthly fee — for now

PKR 35,000 / month
To keep the website alive and working, during this quiet period.

Why 35,000 and not 60,000: the website is not yet open to families (the user side is not active). Right now the work is only to keep it online and fix any problem. Because the work is lighter during this period, the fee is lower — a fair, reduced rate while things are quiet.

Point 2

What this fee covers — and what it does not

✓ Included in 35,000

  • Keeping the website live and online (uptime)
  • Fixing errors and problems that appear
  • Regular backups so no data is lost

✗ Not included (charged separately)

  • New features or new pages
  • Entering or managing data
  • Design changes or redesigns
  • Any new work beyond the above

Why this is written clearly: so there is no confusion later. The 35,000 keeps the website healthy. Anything new or extra is separate work and will be quoted and agreed before it begins.

Point 3

Staff training

1 free training session each month

One staff training session per month is included in the fee. Any additional session is PKR 5,000 each. For any training that requires travel, the Center will arrange the conveyance (transport).

Why: helping staff learn is included once a month. Extra sessions take extra time, so they are charged separately — this is normal and fair.

Point 4

Payment record & schedule

September 2026 — received on 30 September 2026PKR 35,000 ✓ Paid
October 2026 — due on 30 October 2026PKR 35,000 — Due
Going forwardPaid on the 30th of each month

Why this is recorded: so both sides have a clear record of what has been paid and what is next. The monthly fee is paid on the 30th of each month.

Point 5

When the fee returns to 60,000

Once the website is opened to the public and families begin using it (the user side becomes active), the monthly maintenance fee returns to the agreed PKR 60,000 / month.

Why the fee increases then: when real families are using the website every day, there is much more to look after — more traffic, more accounts, payments moving through, and faster support needed. The work becomes heavier, so the fee returns to the full maintenance amount that was originally agreed.
